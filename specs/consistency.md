# Consistency check (specs v0.4)

Generated by reading every file against the others, plus a mechanical check: every backticked
parameter used in a strategy's rules is declared in its own table, or is a shared parameter of
shared.md. Result: no parameter is missing. ORB's `stop_buffer` was implicit in the trailing
rule and is now named explicitly.

## Timing (full day; early closes shift by the session's own close)

| | Setups and candidates from | Last candidate | Cutoff | Why the start slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common (SD-10) | 15 (09:45) | 360 (15:30) | 380 (15:50) | `CURRENT_SESSION` ATR and zone `zv2` |
| VPC | 31 (10:01) | 360 | 380 | `disp_30_atr` for TREND_UP |
| ORB | 16 (09:46) | 120 (11:30) | 380 | OR frozen at slot 15 |
| BRT | 15 | 360 | 380 | Common |
| FBR | 31 | 330 (15:00) | 380 | Trend block needs `disp_30_atr` |
| VMR | 60 (10:30) | 330 | 380 | RANGE needs `crosses_60` |

- Every strategy start is at or after the common earliest slot, and every last-candidate slot is
  at or before the common one. ✓
- Maximum holds (30–60) can run past the cutoff; SESSION_CUTOFF has first precedence (SH-31). ✓
- Decisions on slot m happen at or after `finalized_at(m)` = `bar_end` + 2 s, everywhere. ✓
- On early closes (N = 210), a strategy's `last_entry_slot` is capped by the common last
  candidate, slot 180 (SD-10). FBR and VMR go from 330 to 180; ORB's 120 is unaffected. ✓ (SH-18)

## Identifiers and records

- Market records use the shared-data envelope; strategy records use the strategy-experiment
  envelope (S-1). ✓
- `seq` is unique only within (stream, session); every hash includes `dataset_id`, `session_id`
  and, where relevant, `stream_id` (S-3, S-4). ✓ (SH-21)
- `signal_key` is stable across reruns; `candidate_id` and `evaluation_id` include `run_id`. ✓
  (SH-23)
- Every strategy emits a StrategyEvaluation per slot and a StrategyTransition on change. Non-bar
  causes have null `snapshot_id`. ✓ (SH-25, SH-26)

## Quotas (all consumed at emission, D-09)

| ORB | 1 per direction | The opposite direction stays allowed |

All five share one **reference** position at a time and a 5-slot cooldown, both on the reference model. Working option orders are execution state only. ✓

## Layers (strategy vs execution)

- Every state rule (one position, cooldown, quotas, VMR brake, timers) reads only reference
  positions and market data. No rule reads option positions, fills or P&L. ✓ (SH-26, SH-37–40)
- The reference exit precedence contains no execution-only exit (premium stop, operator flatten,
  operational liquidation). ✓ (D-31)
- Strategy transitions can't be caused by broker or operator events (S-6). ✓
- The `directional_invalidation_brake` reads the **triggered** exit reasons, not the primary one,
  so reporting precedence can't change behaviour. ✓ (VMR-X6)

## Data status

- Entries happen only on `PRESENT` slots, for every strategy. ✓
- `vwap_uncertain` stops only VWAP-dependent strategies (VPC through VWAP, regime and
  displacement; FBR through displacement and its target; VMR through d and regime). ORB and BRT
  are unaffected. ✓ (SH-32)

## Clocks (D-37)

- Strategy timers anchor to `signal_bar_end`; expiry to `scheduled_finalization_at`; telemetry to
  actual times. No rule uses the actual availability time to compute a strategy deadline or an
  expiry. ✓ (SH-20, SH-36, SH-41)
- A late snapshot never changes strategy state; it only makes the candidate not
  submission-eligible. ✓

## Readiness floors

- No setup or candidate before slot 15 (09:45); ORB's first candidate is slot 16; VPC and FBR from
  31; VMR from 60. Regime INCOMPLETE is never treated as RANGE or NEUTRAL. ✓ (SH-42, SH-46)

## Same-slot rules

- Default (SD-13): anchors, then failure, then one stage, then trigger, then deadline.
- The declared exceptions are FBR's same-slot return and VMR's trigger on the confirmation slot.
  Both are listed in D-15. ✓
- Collisions where the intrabar order is unknown resolve conservatively: BRT runaway beats retest;
  FBR's `exc_max` beats a return. ✓

## Known gaps, not inconsistencies

- The trade eligibility matrix (D-04) is still to be written from the CTA and UTP tables.
- Data partition dates (D-02) are the owner's.
- The Step 3 execution schemas are out of scope here (schemas.md).
